One image summing up Monday 23 November 2026: 44 stories on a single
newspaper-style page. Entered in the make.fun bounty "Sum up 23 November's
world news in one image": https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261123

Three hero stories lead the page — the COP31 collapse in Antalya, a boy
pulled alive from a hit residential building in Kyiv, and tankers struck
in the Strait of Hormuz — followed by 10 illustrated cards and a numbered,
region-grouped list of the remaining 31 stories, a small world map and a
date check. Sources: BBC, CNN, Reuters, WSJ, The Hindu, Le Monde,
The Times — 23 Nov 2026.

## Stories

44 stories in total: 3 heroes, 10 illustrated cards, 31 in the numbered
list (Europe 6, Middle East 4, Asia-Pacific 7, Americas 5, Tech &
Business 4, Sport & Culture 5).

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

`node main.ts` copies the included `entry.png` (1024×1024) to
`/output/entry.png`. No dependencies, no network. The output is
deterministic: two runs produce a byte-identical PNG. (`draw.py` is the
PIL script that originally drew the image; it is kept for provenance
and is not the entrypoint.)
